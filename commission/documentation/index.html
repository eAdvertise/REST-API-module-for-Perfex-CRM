Sales Commission

Commission programs in eAD-CRM

Configure commission rules for staff and clients, calculate earnings from paid invoices, review results, and issue commission receipts from one module.

Configure before recalculating. Confirm policy dates, assignment rules, and percentages in a staging environment before applying them to historical invoices.

When an invoice payment is recorded, the module selects the applicable policy and saves the calculated commission. Existing invoices can be processed with the recalculation tool.

Configuration

Create a commission policy

  1. Open Commission → Commission Program.
  2. Select New Commission Program.
  3. Enter a descriptive name and effective start date.
  4. Choose calculation by invoice or product.
  5. Select percentage, fixed amount, or ladder calculation and complete its values.
  6. Save, then assign the policy to staff or clients.

Assign applicable staff

STAFFCommission → Applicable staff
  1. Create an assignment and give it a recognizable name.
  2. Select the commission policy.
  3. Select one or more staff members.
  4. Save the assignment and verify there are no conflicting rules for the same effective period.

Staff receive commission only when they match an active assignment and the related invoice satisfies the selected policy.

Required: creating a commission program does not assign it automatically. Add the invoice sales agent under Commission → Applicable staff, even when that staff member is already selected as the invoice salesperson.

Assign applicable clients

CLIENTCommission → Applicable Client

Client assignments apply a commission policy to selected customers. Use this when a customer, partner, or referral relationship earns commission independently of the salesperson assignment.

Tip: use clear assignment names that include the policy and customer group so overlapping rules are easy to audit.

Recalculate invoices

  1. Open Commission → Statistics.
  2. Select Recalculate.
  3. Choose paid or partially paid invoices that have not been calculated. An invoice without a payment produces no commission amount.
  4. Enable the historical-invoice option only when the current rules should apply to old invoices.
  5. Run the calculation and review the resulting table.
Historical data: recalculation can apply current policy rules. Export or back up existing commission data first.

Create and send commission receipts

  1. Open Commission → Commission receipt.
  2. Create a receipt and select unpaid commission rows.
  3. Enter the payment date, amount, payment mode, and note.
  4. Save to mark the selected commissions as paid.
  5. Open the receipt to download the PDF, email the salesperson, or convert it to an expense.
